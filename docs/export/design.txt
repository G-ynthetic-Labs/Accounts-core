Accounts-core · design note

G-ynthetic Labs · Internal · SIMULATED until the owner's word · 21 Nov 2026

Accounts-core is the user accounts module every G-ynthetic Labs product pins. It covers:

-   user accounts kept on the person's own computer;
-   the ways to sign in;
-   sessions;
-   the telemetry runtime.

Poly (Agent-Advocate-core and its editions) is the first product to use it.

What lives where

The repo map is POL-REPO-001.

  Concern                                                                                                                       Home
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Accounts, sessions, passwords, magic links, what is kept on the computer, the telemetry runtime (consent, counting, export)   Accounts-core (this repository)
  Sign in with Google, Apple, GitHub: proving who the person is and handing over a checked identity                             connectors-lib: sign-in-google, sign-in-apple, sign-in-github, built on its sign-in kit
  The telemetry probe contract and the per-use-case probes                                                                      adapter-lib: telemetry/, plus domains/<product>/ for one product's probes
  Sealing secrets at rest                                                                                                       The product's vault, such as Poly's @poly/vault. Accounts-core seals nothing itself.

Accounts-core holds no adapters and no connectors. A product wires them together:

1.  A connector proves who the person is.
2.  Accounts-core keeps the account.
3.  A probe counts what the person agreed to.

Accounts (@accounts/core)

Storage: the product's vault

-   Every account, session, consent and count is one JSON document.
-   Each document is sealed by the product's vault through the Vault port: put, get, remove, names.
-   get returns a SecretValue with use and wipe. This is the same shape as Poly's vault.
-   vaultProblems(vault) is a conformance check. A product runs it against its own vault.
-   Item names are lower-case kebab-case, at most 64 characters, so they round-trip through Poly's
    vault.

Audit log

-   Every change is first written to the product's audit log as asked, then as done or refused with
    its codes.
-   If the audit log will not take the asked entry, nothing changes (A402).
-   The audit log holds:
    -   the action;
    -   the account id;
    -   the stage;
    -   the codes;
    -   the time.
-   It never holds an address, a provider's sub, a token or a password.
-   The audit log is tamper-evident through the product's hash chain. It is never called
    tamper-proof.

Identities

-   A sign-in arrives as { provider, sub, email?, email_verified }, using the OpenID Connect claim
    names.
-   The provider's sub is never stored as it came. It is kept as a keyed hash (HMAC-SHA256) under a
    pepper sealed in the vault.
-   Emails are kept masked, except for an email account's own address.
-   One account can link several sign-ins.
-   The last sign-in on an account cannot be unlinked (A204).
-   A verified magic link reaching an account whose email was unverified marks that sign-in verified
    and clears the password.

Sessions

-   A session token is 32 random bytes.
-   The vault keeps only its SHA-256 digest.
-   create, validate, revoke and revokeAll manage sessions.
-   How long a session lasts is the owner's sessionMinutes.

Passwords

-   Only a scrypt password hash is kept, in the form scrypt$N$r$p$salt$hash (RFC 7914).
-   Lockout follows the owner's lockoutThreshold and lockoutMinutes.
-   Every scrypt number and the minimum length are the owner's.

Who may change things

-   Only a person creates, renames, links, unlinks or removes an account, sets a password, or signs
    out (D-145, D-208).
-   An assistant is refused (A110).

Email ways in (@accounts/email)

-   Email and password. Two functions handle it:
    -   createUserWithEmailAndPassword makes the account. The email is not verified this way, and
        the account says so.
    -   signInWithEmailAndPassword signs in to it.
-   Magic links, which are optional and off until the owner turns them on:
    -   The token is 32 bytes. Only its digest is kept.
    -   A link works once and is removed before anything is returned.
    -   The token travels in the link's fragment.
    -   The link opens only at the owner's linkBase: a private-use scheme, loopback with a port, or
        https.
    -   Sending goes through a MailSender port. SimulatedOutbox stands in until the owner chooses
        the service.
    -   A rate limiter caps how many links one address may be sent (the owner's maxSends per
        windowMinutes).
    -   If sending fails, the link is revoked and the rate limit released.
    -   The answer never says whether an account exists.

Telemetry (@accounts/telemetry)

Probes

-   Probes come from adapter-lib and are checked against its contract before they run.
-   A probe declares counters, each with a description and at most one attribute of fixed values.
    This follows OpenTelemetry's counter and attribute in name only.
-   A probe also declares a disclosure: what it collects, why, and what it never collects.

Consent

-   Consent is per person and per probe.
-   It is off by default and given for one probe version, so a new version is asked for again.
-   Saying no deletes what that probe counted for the person.
-   Only a person gives or withdraws consent.

Counting

-   The meter hands each local event to the probes the person agreed to.
-   It keeps only what the contract accepts, by export interval (the owner's exportIntervalDays).
-   Nothing is counted while telemetry is off (T110).

Export

-   A person sees exactly what would be sent (preview) and sends it (export). An assistant cannot.
-   Batches hold only ended intervals and are added up across everyone on the computer who agreed.
-   Every series below the owner's suppressionThreshold is left out, and only how many were left out
    is said.
-   Batches hold no account, person or time beyond the interval's days.
-   A batch with every series suppressed is dropped and never sent.
-   If the exporter fails, nothing is lost (T200).
-   Every batch sent is kept in the history.

Numbers

Every number is the owner's, and an unset number refuses (A100 and A101):

-   session length;
-   the scrypt numbers, minimum length and lockout;
-   link life, sends and window;
-   export interval and suppression threshold.

None is invented here. The tests use made-up values only.

Checks

npm run check runs:

-   the type check;
-   the code map, which covers 1×3×n modules, line limits and features;
-   the legal notice;
-   the test keys;
-   the internal-words check;
-   every test.

The test keys are ACC-T, EML-T and TEL-T. The tests run against a simulated vault, audit log, outbox
and exporter, with synthetic .test people only.
